# Lesson 1: Numbers, Division and Modulo

## Idea

Modulo gives the remainder after division.

Think of a 12-hour clock:

- 13 o'clock becomes 1
- 25 o'clock also becomes 1

So:

```python
13 % 12 == 1
25 % 12 == 1
```

## Where it is useful

- odd and even checks
- circular arrays
- clocks and rotations
- repeating patterns
